WHAT IT DOES

WHAT IT DOES

One receptionist. Zero missed calls.

Every patient who calls or messages gets an instant, helpful answer, even when your team is with a patient or the practice is closed.

Feature 01

Answers Every Call (24/7)

Voice and chat, day and night. Evenings, weekends and busy mornings are covered, so new patients never reach a dead end or a voicemail.

Feature 02

Books Appointments

Direct to your calendar. The agent qualifies each patient, offers only open slots and books the appointment without any phone tag.

Feature 03

Handles FAQs

Prices, hours, services. The questions that tie up reception are answered instantly and accurately, and anything complex is passed to your team.

HOW IT WORKS

HOW IT WORKS

From first call to booked appointment.

The AI Receptionist handles the whole journey, so your team only sees patients who are ready to come in.

Step 01

Patient calls or chats

Every call and website chat is picked up instantly, 24/7, by the voice and chat agent.

Step 02

AI answers and qualifies

It answers questions on prices, hours and services, and finds out what the patient needs.

Step 03

Books into your calendar

It offers open slots and books the appointment directly, with no phone tag.

Step 04

Your team stays in control

Anything clinical or complex is handed to your team with the patient’s details and a summary of the conversation.
